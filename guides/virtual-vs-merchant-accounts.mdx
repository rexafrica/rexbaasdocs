---
title: Virtual Accounts vs. Merchant Accounts
description: Two account types, two different jobs — how to pick
---

Both are dedicated Rex bank account numbers you can issue under your own
business. They are not two tiers of the same thing — they're built for
different jobs, and one of them structurally cannot do what the other does.

## Virtual Accounts

Use this for **end users you want to give a wallet to** — the general-purpose
account type. A Virtual Account can:

- Send and receive money ([Transfer Between Accounts](/api-reference/virtual-accounts/transfer-between-rex-issued-accounts),
  [Transfer to Another Bank](/api-reference/virtual-accounts/transfer-to-another-bank))
- Receive inbound transfers from other banks
- Be posted to directly ([Post a Manual Credit or Debit](/api-reference/virtual-accounts/post-a-manual-credit-or-debit))

No subscription required — available as soon as your partner account is provisioned.

## Merchant Accounts

Use this for **collecting payments at a terminal or point of sale** — a
narrower, purpose-built account type. A Merchant Account:

- **Can receive money, and can never send it.** There's no transfer/send
  endpoint for this account type at all — it isn't a permission you can
  toggle, the capability doesn't exist in the API surface. If you need an
  account that can pay out, that's what a Virtual Account is for.
- Is always funded from your business Master balance, never from a
  "virtual account FBO" the way Virtual Accounts are internally.
- Can optionally be tied to a specific `terminal_id` you already have
  registered with Rex.
- **Requires a service subscription first** —
  [Subscribe to a Service](/api-reference/services/subscribe-to-a-service) —
  before [Create a Merchant Account](/api-reference/merchant-accounts/create-a-merchant-account)
  will succeed. Skipping this returns `403 NOT_SUBSCRIBED`.

## Quick decision

| If you need to... | Use |
|---|---|
| Give an end user a wallet they can send from | Virtual Account |
| Collect payment at a till/terminal, no outgoing transfers | Merchant Account |
| Both send and receive on the same account | Virtual Account — Merchant Accounts can't send |
